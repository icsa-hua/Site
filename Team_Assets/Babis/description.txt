this service is a grocery assistant whose role is twofold. First to obey voice
commands to include groceries on the shopping kart and secondly to inform about
groceries dropped prices and about product offers. The assistant is called Babi
it is a lightweight LLM that also uses another model for voice generation, as the
service supports that. Babi will speak to state that he did the command we told
it to. The interface captured in the screenshots, is simplistic, and the products
include a bunch of groceries, milk, yogurt, salads and more healthy foods. In
general the main point of this service is to promote healthy meals and babi is a
way to assist people to make their shopping in an efficient but also economy
friendly way.
