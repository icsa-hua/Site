Human vs AI

«Βρες το κουτάλι» — a find-the-object race between human eyes and a vision model.

1 About

A head-to-head "spot the object" game: you and a vision model are both prompted to find the same item hidden somewhere in an image, and both are timed. Five rounds, each harder than the last — a cluttered desk full of near-identical objects, a scene shrouded in heavy fog, and other visually tricky setups designed to challenge one side more than the other. After five images, the scores are tallied and compared. The goal is to make a trade-off tangible: vision models are often dramatically faster than human perception, but they aren't infallible — this game is a quick, visual way to see both the speed and the failure modes side by side.

2 Preview

The main interface: a live score line (you vs. the model) above the round counter, with the target image in the center of the screen.
