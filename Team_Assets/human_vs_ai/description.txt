The human vs ai game is a simple game as well, that pits each other in a find
waldo-type of game. Basically it prompts the user and the vision model to find
random objects inside the shown images. With each level the image becomes harder,
for either the human or the vision model. For instance the third image is a very
    cluttered desk with many similar objects and the prompt requests an item. On
    another occasion the ai model has to find something inside an image that
    shows heavy fog. Then after 5 images, the final score is pitched. 

The main goal for this asset is to show how fast the vision models are in
comparison to human perception and eye sight, but also show that they are not
perfect, essentially the trade-off between accuracy and efficiency. The
screenshot shows the main interface, and then the other thing shown in the
display is the image in the center of the screen. Again simple design but
efficient.
