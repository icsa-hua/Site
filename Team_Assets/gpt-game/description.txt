this assets focuses on showing to different age-groups how the LLMs formulate
phrases. Depending on the age group the phrases become more elaborate and also
the answers reveal how the LLM thinks. The screenshots show exactly that change
between ages. The focus of this asset is to help younger ages understand how an
LLM "thinks" and understands the meaning of the phrase. This is a simplistic game
and the design is also simple to give focues to the questions and the choices the
user has to make. The users in the different categories can change the
temperature of the model so that the answers change and become more random or
more crazy.
